The house

We make fewer watches than we are asked for.

Last year we turned down more orders than we filled. It is not a marketing line. A watch that takes six months to build cannot be hurried to meet a demand curve, and we would rather disappoint a buyer than a wrist.

Every GLX piece carries a numbered movement, a hand-finished case and a signed certificate from the bench where it was born.
