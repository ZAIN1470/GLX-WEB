Services

The watch is only half the purchase.

What you are really buying is the promise that someone will look after it for the rest of your life.

Our commitments

What every owner receives.

Warranty

Five years, in writing

Covers manufacturing defects in movement, case and dial. No small print about "normal wear" that excuses a fault. Register online or at any boutique.

Service

Every five years

We recommend a full service at five-year intervals. The watch is disassembled, cleaned, re-oiled, regulated and tested for water resistance. Typical turnaround is six weeks.

Restoration

For as long as it runs

We keep parts for every calibre we have ever made. If a component has been discontinued, our workshop will cut a new one rather than send you away.

How a service works

Simple, and transparent.

  1. Contact us

    Write or visit a boutique. We will record the serial number and any concerns you have about how the watch is running.

  2. Quoted before we begin

    A watchmaker examines it and sends a fixed quote. Nothing is done until you approve it, and nothing is added without asking.

  3. Returned, with a report

    Your watch comes back with a timing printout and a note on what was replaced. Old parts are returned on request.

Guidance

Looking after it between visits.

  • Keep it away from magnetsPhones, speakers, bag clasps
  • Wind gentlyThirty turns if it has stopped
  • Rinse after salt waterFresh water, crown fully pushed in
  • Store flat or in a winderNot in direct sunlight
  • Do not change the datebetween 9 pm and 3 am

Is your watch due?

Send us the serial number and we will tell you when it was last serviced and what we suggest.
