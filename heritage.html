Heritage

It began with a repair bench.

GLX did not start as a brand. It started as a small workshop that fixed other people's watches, and learned from every one.

In 1998, Ghazanfar L. Khan opened a two-room repair shop above a stationery store in Saddar. He trained in Geneva, came home with a set of tools and a stubborn opinion: that the watches arriving on his bench were fine, but they could have been better.

For fifteen years he restored other makers' work. He learned where movements failed, which finishes wore through, and which shortcuts collectors discovered only after the warranty ran out. A notebook from those years is still in the workshop, full of small complaints and sketches.

In 2013 he built the first GLX for himself. Friends asked for one. Then their friends. The name came from his initials, plus an X for the thing he could not quite name but kept chasing.
